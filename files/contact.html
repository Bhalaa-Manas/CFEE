Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept reservations?

We're a walk-in café but for events or large groups (8+), please contact us at events@cfee.in to pre-book a section.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes! You can order online directly from our website. We deliver within 5 km of each store. Delivery charge is ₹30.

Do you have vegan options?

Absolutely! All our teas, cold brew, and black coffees are vegan. We also offer oat milk and almond milk alternatives for an additional ₹20.

Can I host a private event at Cfee?

Yes! We host birthday parties, corporate gatherings, workshops, and more. Email events@cfee.in with your requirements.

Is there Wi-Fi available?

Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available at all our locations. Ask a barista for the password.
